02 // MATHEMATICAL THEOREM

Quantum Data Re-uploading & Fourier Spectrum

Why standard variational quantum circuits fail on complex tabular data, and how repeated encoding creates universal function approximators.

SCHULD ET AL. (2021) LIMITATION

A quantum circuit with a single initial feature encoding unitary \(U(x) = W \cdot S(x)\) is mathematically equivalent to a truncated Fourier series of degree 1:

f(x) = ∑ c_n · e^(i · n · x), where spectrum Ω = {-1, 0, +1}

Single-layer VQCs cannot resolve sharp tabular boundaries or multi-modal network attack vectors.

PÉREZ-SALINAS (2020) DATA RE-UPLOADING

Interleaving classical feature rotations \(S_l(x)\) between variational layers \(W_l(\theta)\) expands the harmonic spectrum linearly with depth \(L\):

U(x, θ) = ∏_{l=1}^L [ W_l(θ) · S_l(x) ] → Ω_L = {-L, ..., +L}

Each layer \(L\) unlocks higher harmonic frequencies, creating complex multi-dimensional decision boundaries on 4 qubits.
